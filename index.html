The AJEESH ROOM presents :
AI-Ready MSME Roundtable

The Ajeesh Room is an exclusive, invitation-only leadership forum bringing together policy makers, enterprise leaders, technology providers, financial institutions, workforce bodies, and quality & standards organizations to address one critical challenge: How do India's MSMEs actually become AI-ready?

The roundtable goes beyond technology alone, examining the real conditions required for adoption—affordable solutions, access to finance, workforce readiness, compliance frameworks, enterprise expectations, and enabling policy.

The outcome is the AI-Ready MSME Blueprint: a practical roadmap identifying key priorities, critical gaps, and enablers to help MSMEs adopt AI effectively, scale with confidence, and remain competitive in an increasingly technology-led economy.
